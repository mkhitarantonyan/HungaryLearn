## L1 · sounds and recognition

- **CORE (6):** `gyerek`, `kutya`, `szép`, `sok`, `üveg`, `alma` — the unchanged runtime set.
- **FUNCTIONAL CHUNK (0):** greetings are recognition material here and become functional in L2.
- **RECEPTIVE (7):** `szia`, `sör`, `család`, `nyár`, `kenyér`, `zsák`, `folyó`.
- **RECYCLED (0):** first lesson.
- Decision: no RolePlay. A simulated conversation would exceed the phonics/recognition can-do; decoding plus short open writing is the honest progression.

## L2 · greeting and identity

- **CORE (4):** `köszönöm`, `lenni`, `én`, `magyar`.
- **FUNCTIONAL CHUNK (5):** `Szia!`, `Jó napot!`, `Viszlát!`, `Hogy vagy?`, `A nevem…`.
- **RECEPTIVE (3):** `Ön`, `Örülök`, `diák`.
- **RECYCLED (0):** L1 greeting strings are promoted here to functional use, so they are counted once as L2 chunks rather than twice.

## L3 · objects, articles, plural

- **CORE (6):** `a`, `az`, `egy`, `ház`, `könyv`, `asztal` — unchanged runtime entries.
- **FUNCTIONAL CHUNK (4):** `Mi ez?`, `Mi az?`, `Mik ezek?`, `Mik azok?`.
- **RECEPTIVE (5):** `szék`, `ablak`, `ajtó`, `telefon`, `füzet`.
- **RECYCLED (6):** `Szia!`, `köszönöm`, `itt`, `ott`, `van`, `vannak`.
- Boundary: no accusative and no new Listening. L3's learner-facing Listening gap remains intentional until a later approved audio package.

## L4 · common present actions

- **CORE (7):** `tanul`, `olvas`, `ír`, `beszél`, `dolgozik`, `lakik`, `nem` — unchanged runtime set.
- **FUNCTIONAL CHUNK (4):** `Mit csinálsz?`, `Dolgozol vagy tanulsz?`, `Reggel…`, `Este…`.
- **RECEPTIVE (4):** `iszik`, `eszik`, `hallgat`, `pihen`.
- **RECYCLED (8):** `Szia!`, `magyar`, `könyv`, `asztal`, `itt`, `egy`, `te`, `Anna`.
- Boundary: the activities stay within the current indefinite-present scope; full definite conjugation is not introduced.

## L5 · numbers, days, time, scheduling

- **CORE (10):** `kettő`, `két`, `tizen-`, `huszon-`, `száz`, `hétfő`, `hónap`, `dátum`, `perc`, `másodperc`.
- **FUNCTIONAL CHUNK (3):** `Hány óra van?`, `háromkor`, `Mikor érsz rá?`.
- **RECEPTIVE (4):** `kezdődik`, `szabad`, `találkozunk`, `holnap`.
- **RECYCLED (8):** `tanul`, `dolgozik`, `olvas`, `ír`, `nem`, `igen`, `Szia!`, `Viszlát!`.
- Boundary: only full-hour `óra van` / `-kor` scheduling is productive; no premature colloquial quarter/half-hour system is added.

## L6 · integrated recycling

- **CORE (0), FUNCTIONAL CHUNK (0), RECEPTIVE (0):** P1 intentionally adds no new L6 vocabulary target.
- **RECYCLED (6 runtime anchors):** `Szia!`, `Hogy vagy?`, `könyv`, `tanulok`, `hétfő`, `dolgozik`.
- The integrated text and activities reuse the bounded L1–L5 inventory. L6 is a checkpoint, not a vocabulary dump and not CEFR certification.

## Later vocabulary/audio package candidates

Candidates that may deserve runtime SRS only after lexicographic review and physical word audio exist: `Örülök`, `szék`, `ablak`, `telefon`, `füzet`, `iszik`, `eszik`, `pihen`, `kezdődik`, `szabad`, `találkozunk`, `holnap`. P1 does not publish these as new runtime cards.
