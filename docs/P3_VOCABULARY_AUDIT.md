## Runtime and audio boundary

- P3 adds no runtime vocabulary card, vocabulary ID, compatibility alias, `wordAudioMap` entry, or word MP3.
- Useful later audio/SRS candidates are the route-repair chunks in L12 and the routine questions in L14. They remain lesson content until dedicated word audio is approved.
- L11 intentionally has no learner-facing Listening activity or dedicated Listening MP3 in the current curriculum. P3 documents this gap and does not substitute slide narration, word audio, browser TTS, or a fabricated asset.
- Existing published Listening A assets for L12–L14 remain the only runtime Listening assets in these lessons.
